### 💡 One Recommendation

I would keep **two separate documents** going forward:

1. **Future Enhancement Roadmap** *(the curated list above)* – strategic product features planned after the MVP.
2. **Backend API Improvement Report** – a living document that we'll update after each sprint with missing endpoints, API enhancements, and integration requirements.

This separation keeps the product vision and backend engineering tasks independent, making both much easier to maintain as DermAssist AI grows.
