That's actually an excellent idea. 👍

Let's maintain an **"Enhancement Backlog"**. The rule will be:

* **Phase 3:** Build the application.
* **Backlog:** Capture every good idea.
* **After Phase 3:** Prioritize and implement the best ideas.

This prevents scope creep while ensuring no good idea gets lost.

# One Rule for This Backlog

We'll tag every idea with a priority:

* 🔴 **P1** – Must Have
* 🟡 **P2** – Should Have
* 🟢 **P3** – Nice to Have

This will help us decide what to implement after Phase 3 without getting distracted during the current sprint.

I think keeping this backlog is one of the best decisions for the project—it lets us stay disciplined while continuously capturing ideas that can make DermAssist AI stand out.
